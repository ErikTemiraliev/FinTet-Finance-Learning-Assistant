Simple Interest:
Simple interest is interest calculated only on the original principal. 
Formula: Interest = Principal × Rate × Time.

Compound Interest:
Compound interest is interest calculated on both the original principal and accumulated interest.
Formula: Future Value = Principal × (1 + Rate)^Time.

Interest Rate:
Interest rate is the percentage charged or earned on money over a period of time.

Risk and Return:
Risk means the possibility of losing money or receiving lower returns than expected.
Return means the profit or income earned from an investment.
Usually, higher expected return comes with higher risk.

Stock:
A stock represents ownership in a company. Stockholders may earn money from price increases or dividends.

Bond:
A bond is a debt instrument. When investors buy bonds, they lend money to the issuer. The issuer promises to pay interest and return the principal.

Financial Market:
A financial market is a place where people and institutions trade financial assets such as stocks, bonds, and currencies.

Investment:
Investment means putting money into assets in order to earn future income or profit.

Present Value:
Present value is the current value of future money after discounting.

Future Value:
Future value is the amount of money that an investment will grow to in the future.
